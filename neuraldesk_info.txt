NeuralDesk — AI Chatbot Builder for Your Business

WHAT IS NEURALDESK?
NeuralDesk is a RAG-as-a-Service platform that lets any business create AI chatbots trained on their own data. Upload your documents, connect your database, or paste a website URL — and get a smart chatbot on your website in minutes. No coding required.

FEATURES:
- 34+ Data Sources: Upload PDF, DOCX, Excel, CSV, JSON, TXT, HTML files. Crawl any website URL. Connect MySQL, MongoDB, PostgreSQL, SQLite databases.
- Any LLM Provider: Use OpenAI GPT-4o, Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, Mistral, Groq, or run completely local and free with Ollama.
- One-Line Embed: Copy one script tag, paste it on your website HTML before the closing body tag. The chatbot widget appears instantly on your site.
- Smart Analytics: See what customers ask most, track unanswered questions, measure satisfaction with thumbs up/down, capture visitor leads automatically.
- Lead Capture: Collect visitor name and email before chat starts. Export leads to your CRM.
- Private and Secure: Run on your own servers. Your data never leaves your network. Full control over privacy.
- Bot Templates: Choose from 6+ pre-built templates — Customer Support, Sales Assistant, HR Helper, Documentation Bot, E-Commerce, or start from scratch.
- Schema-First Pipeline: When you upload databases or spreadsheets, NeuralDesk auto-cleans the data, detects types, builds a glossary, and gets your confirmation before processing.

HOW IT WORKS:
Step 1: Sign up for free at neuraldesk.com
Step 2: Create a new bot — choose a template or start from scratch
Step 3: Upload your data — PDF, DOCX, Excel, CSV, or paste a website URL
Step 4: Configure — choose your LLM provider, set bot personality, customize colors
Step 5: Deploy — copy one line of embed code and paste it on your website
Your AI chatbot is now live and answering customer questions 24/7.

FAQ:
Q: Do I need coding skills?
A: No. NeuralDesk is completely no-code. Upload files, configure settings, copy embed code — all through a visual dashboard.

Q: Is my data safe?
A: Yes. You can run NeuralDesk on your own servers. Data never leaves your infrastructure. We support local LLMs via Ollama for complete privacy.

Q: Can I use my own API keys?
A: Yes. On Pro plan and above, you can use your own OpenAI, Gemini, Claude, or other API keys.

Q: How fast are the responses?
A: With cloud LLMs like Groq, responses are under 1 second. With local Ollama models, 3-10 seconds depending on your hardware.

Q: Can I customize the chatbot appearance?
A: Yes. Change colors, bot name, welcome message, suggested questions, and position on your website.

Q: What happens if the bot cannot answer?
A: It will say it can only answer from its knowledge base. You can also see unanswered questions in the analytics dashboard and upload more content to cover those topics.

Q: Can I connect my database?
A: Yes. Connect MySQL, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, or upload a SQLite file. NeuralDesk auto-profiles the data, cleans it, and builds a schema.
